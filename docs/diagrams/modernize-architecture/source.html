A gRPC-only service, reachable from a browser

Four Deployments on OpenShift. The service speaks gRPC and nothing else; Envoy synthesises the REST surface from the service's own .proto, and a browser page calls it over plain JSON. Every name, port and count on this page was read from the running cluster.
